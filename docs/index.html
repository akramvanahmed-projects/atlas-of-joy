People together vs happiness beyond money

Each dot is a country. Up means happier than its GDP per head would predict. Right means more of its local photos show people together: friends, family, parties, weddings, crowds.

Which signals matter

Black bar: rank correlation with happiness once wealth is taken out. Grey dot: plain correlation. 67 countries with GDP data.

How to read this

The photos were taken between 2004 and 2014 by people photographing their own country. The happiness scores come from the same years.

This is a correlation across 69 countries, not cause and effect. Photographing friends doesn't make a country happier. More likely, places where social life is visible and valued score higher on both.

Flickr users aren't a random slice of any country. They are probably younger, better off and more urban than average, and the gap is likely bigger in poorer countries.

We tested 18 signals. Some of them will look good by luck. The people signals survive a placebo test, but treat them as a lead, not a law.
